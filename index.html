What EnergyPulse Does

Real-Time Monitoring

Track energy consumption across the campus down to the second. Instantly spot anomalies and spikes.

Cloud Analytics

Leverage Firebase Cloud for deep historical analysis, predictive modeling, and demand forecasting.

Smart Alerts

Get instant notifications for unusual usage patterns, equipment failures, or when limits are exceeded.

Device Tracking

Monitor individual high-load devices, HVAC systems, and server rooms for granular insights.

Cost Optimization

Identify wastage, optimize peak-hour usage, and automatically reduce energy expenditure.

Carbon Footprint

Automatically calculate and track your campus CO₂ emissions to meet sustainability goals.
